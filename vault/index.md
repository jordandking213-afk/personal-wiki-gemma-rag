## Topics from Romans 8
- [[Four Spiritual Freedoms]] — Using Wiersbe's commentary, the notes divide Romans 8 into four spiritual freedoms.
- [[Three Groans of Suffering]] — Romans 8:18-30 addresses suffering and how believers are free from discouragement.
- [[Hope and Assurance]] — Believers have hope because they know the climax of adoption is coming, and faith is the assurance of things hoped for.
- [[God's Perfect Plan]] — God is always at work (verse 28) and has a perfect plan (verse 29) for those who love Him and are called according to His purpose.
