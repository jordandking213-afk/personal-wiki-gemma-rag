# Results of Rejection

## Summary
Romans 10:18-21 describes the results: Israel is guilty because they did hear, the message is shared with the Gentiles because of Israel's rejection, and God still yearns for His people.

## Key Points
- Israel is guilty; they DID hear.
- Message is shared with Gentiles due to Israel rejection.
- God still yearns for His people.
